## Notes

- The dump also lists a large stats block (Crit, Armor, FlatArmorPen, AbilityHaste, shields, bounding radius, spellbook, AIManager). Only the fields the reader uses were transcribed.
- `IS_TYPE_RVA = 0x293FD0` (`GameObject__IsType`) is in the dump. The reader filters by team id instead, because that costs one read rather than a kernel call per object. Switching to `IsType` would be more precise and slower.
- Turkish-language replies around the dump report several spell/minion-manager patterns as `NOT_FOUND` on this patch, so the spell and minion parts of the graph are less reliable than the object graph.
- The object array is a begin/end pair, not base+count. A half-built array during loading gives `end <= begin`, which the reader treats as "not ready".
- Nothing was verified live. Treat every row as a hypothesis until `demo.py info` returns `MZ` at the base and a plausible object count.
